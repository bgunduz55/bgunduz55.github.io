Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

midnight is a private application used only by its owner, Burak Gündüz, to manage his own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Data accessed

Channel and video metadata, analytics reports, captions and thumbnails of the owner's own channels.

Use and storage

Data is used only to analyse and update the owner's own channels. OAuth tokens and reports are stored locally on the owner's computers. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

midnight's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored data is then deleted on request.
